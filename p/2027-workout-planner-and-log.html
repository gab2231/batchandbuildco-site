    What’s inside

    • Weekly Plan: Mon–Sun focus, main work notes, planned minutes and a done flag
    • Workout Log: one row per set (date, workout name, exercise, set #, reps, weight, optional RPE, volume and notes); 500 rows. Volume = reps × weight
    • Exercise Library: 48 starter exercises with muscle group, equipment and default sets; names feed the log dropdown
    • Progress: best weight, reps at that weight, Epley estimated 1RM (when reps are 1–12 and weight > 0), best single-set volume, times logged and last date
    • Dashboard: sessions, sets and volume for the last 7 and 30 days, yearly set count, weekly goal progress and a chart of exercise set counts
    • Body Metrics: optional date, body weight, waist, chest, hips, sleep hours and notes
    • Settings: weight unit (lb or kg), weekly session goal, optional "as of" date, muscle groups and equipment lists
