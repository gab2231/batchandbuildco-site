Handmade Pricing Calculator Spreadsheet

Handmade Pricing Calculator Spreadsheet: Know what each item costs and what to charge. Type what you paid for each pack of supplies once, list what goes into one of each product, then add your minutes, packaging, shipping and where you sell it. The calculator works out the total cost of one item, a wholesale and a retail guide, and the price that reaches the profit you want after fees.

What’s inside

  • Settings: shop name, your hourly rate, the wholesale multiplier (× total cost) and retail multiplier (× wholesale), 2 and 2 to start, and up to 8 fee profiles: transaction %, processing %, fixed fee per order and listing fee
  • Materials: 100 rows: material, supplier, pack price, units in pack and unit; cost per unit = pack price ÷ units
  • Recipes: 300 lines: product, material and quantity for one item; unit, cost per unit and line cost are looked up
  • Products: 50 rows: fee profile, minutes, packaging, other costs, shipping charged and paid, target profit and your price; materials, your time, total cost, wholesale and retail guides, fee %, price for target profit, fees, profit, margin and hourly earned are worked out
  • Dashboard: products priced, average and lowest margin, products below target profit, average hourly earned, materials, recipe lines, items to fix and every priced product by margin, lowest first
  • Check cells flag a pack with no quantity, a material or product name used twice, a recipe line for a product or material that is not listed, text or negative amounts, a missing fee profile and fees of 100% or more
