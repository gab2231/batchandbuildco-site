What’s inside

  • Start here: how a cook night goes, what to do if you miss one, and a timed plan for the first (busiest) Sunday.
  • The rotation: a 4-week table of what to cook each Sunday and Wednesday and what to eat each day, plus "same base, different finish" ideas so the repeats don't get boring.
  • A simple macro guide: what a typical day adds up to (roughly 1,500–2,100 calories and 110–150 g protein, all estimates) and a table for scaling portions up or down.
  • 9 recipe pages: ingredients with amounts, steps, how to pack and reheat, swaps, and rough calorie and macro ranges.
  • Grocery lists by store section: a weekly staple run, the full Week 1 list, and add-ons for weeks 2–4.
  • Shopping notes, what to freeze each Sunday and an allergen note (the set uses dairy, eggs, fish, wheat, soy, peanuts and sesame).
  • Storage and reheating: fridge and freezer times, food-safety basics and reheating notes.
  • Recipe index, "after day 30" ideas and a cook-night checklist.
