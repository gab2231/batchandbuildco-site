What’s inside

  • Subscriptions: 150 rows for service, category, cost per charge, billing cycle, a charge date, status (Active, Free trial, Paused, Canceled), paid with, who uses it, how often you use it and Keep / Review / Cancel. Formulas fill in per month, per year, next charge, days until and an alert
  • Billing cycles: weekly, every 2 weeks, every 4 weeks, monthly, every 2 months, quarterly, every 6 months and yearly, plus 2 rows for your own (for example every 2 years). Monthly charges on the 29th-31st move to the last day of shorter months
  • Dashboard: active count, per month and per year, budget left (if you set one), due in the next 7 days, free trials running, marked to cancel, saved by canceling, rarely-used cost, the next 12 charges in date order, and totals by category, payment method, person and decision, with 2 charts
  • Renewal Calendar: a month grid with the amount due each day and a date-ordered list of that month's charges (up to 40). Pick any month on Settings, or leave it blank for this month
  • Year Ahead: expected charges for each of the next 12 months, row by row, so yearly and quarterly renewals show up in the month they land
  • Price Changes: log the old and new price to see the change per charge, % change and what it adds per year
  • Settings: an optional "as of" date, due-soon and free-trial alert windows, a monthly budget, and editable categories, payment methods and people for the dropdowns
