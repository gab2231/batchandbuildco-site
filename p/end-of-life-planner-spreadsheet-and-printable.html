What’s inside

  • Settings: your name, an optional "as of" date, how often to review (12 months to start), the bills window (14 days), where the planner is kept and who knows
  • Dashboard: contacts, documents on file, accounts and listed value, bills due soon, digital accounts, wishes answered, last and next review, items to fix, and lists of bills due soon, documents to get or review and accounts to check
  • Contacts: 60 rows: name, role, relationship, phone, email and whether they know where the planner is
  • Documents: 60 rows with 22 common documents listed: have it, where the original is, copy, prepared by, dates and a status (to get, add where it is, review due)
  • Accounts: 80 rows: institution, type, ownership, last 4 digits only, primary and contingent beneficiaries, the date you confirmed them, a rough value and a check
  • Debts & Bills: 60 rows: how often, due day or next due date, autopay, paid from, balance, the next due date and due soon
  • Digital Accounts: 80 rows: username, where the password is kept (never the password), legacy contact setting and what you want done
  • Wishes, Executor Checklist, Reviews: 28 wishes questions plus your own, 22 executor steps with their sources, and a review log
