Puppy Training Tracker Spreadsheet: See your puppy's progress in numbers. Log each potty trip as Outside or Inside, each short training session as tries and successes, and each new person, sound or place your puppy meets. What’s inside

  • Settings: name, breed, birth date, came-home date, an "as of" date and your goals (90% outside, 80% success over at least 20 tries a week and a 14-day "Due soon" window to start)
  • Daily Schedule: up to 30 rows of your routine with times and a type (Potty, Meal, Water, Play, Training, Walk, Nap, Crate, Bedtime, Other), with a count of each type
  • Potty Log: up to 1000 trips: date, time, Pee / Poop / Both / Nothing and Outside or Inside
  • Skills + Training Log: up to 40 skills and 600 sessions (date, skill, tries, successes, minutes); sessions, tries and success % for the last 7 days and all time, last practiced and a status: Reliable, Practicing or Not started
  • Socialization: 60 rows filled with 56 editable ideas (people, animals, sounds, surfaces, places, handling, things, home): first date, times, reaction (Happy, Curious, Unsure, Scared) and notes
  • Health: up to 200 rows: date, type, what, given by, next due date from your vet and cost; age in weeks, days to next due and status (Done, Overdue, Due soon or Upcoming)
  • Weight: up to 100 weigh-ins with age in weeks, change since last and a chart
  • Dashboard: age, days home, outside % and accidents for the last 7 days, sessions and minutes, skills reliable, socialization done, health items due, items to fix, the last 14 days of potty trips with a chart, training by skill and what is coming up from the vet
  • Check cells flag a date or time that is not one, a missing result or Outside/Inside, a skill not on the Skills tab or listed twice, tries that are not a whole number, successes above the tries, a reaction without a date, a weigh-in or vet date before the birth date, two weigh-ins on one date and goals typed as 90 instead of 90%
