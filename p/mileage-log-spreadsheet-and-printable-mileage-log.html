Mileage Log Spreadsheet and Printable Mileage Log

Mileage Log Spreadsheet and Printable Mileage Log: 2026 has two IRS business mileage rates: 72.5 cents a mile through June 30 and 76 cents from July 1. This mileage log looks up the right one for each trip date, so you don't have to. Type the date, car, category, where you went and why, and either the odometer readings or the miles. The rate, the amount and your totals are worked out.

What’s inside

  • Settings: tax year, an optional "as of" date, the "due soon" window for services, and up to 6 vehicles with Jan 1 and Dec 31 odometer readings
  • Trip Log: 1,000 rows: date, vehicle, category (business, medical, charity, military moving, commute, personal), from, to, business purpose, odometer readings or miles with a round-trip option, then the IRS rate for that date, the amount, parking and tolls, and a check column that flags missing details and overlapping odometer readings
  • IRS Rates: the IRS standard mileage rates by period from 2022 through Dec 31, 2026, with room to add 2027 when the IRS announces it
  • Monthly Report: pick a month, a vehicle and a category for a print-ready list of up to 80 trips with totals, plus an optional reimbursement rate
  • Fuel Log: 500 rows: gallons, price and cost, with MPG from one full tank to the next (partial fills counted)
  • Maintenance Log: 500 rows: date, odometer, service, details, shop and cost
  • Service Schedule: 60 rows: type the intervals from your owner's manual and the last service, next due mileage and date, and a Due / Due soon / OK status are worked out from the Maintenance Log
  • Car Expenses: 500 rows: insurance, registration, repairs, tires and other costs, with a business-related column
  • Tax Summary: the tax year by vehicle: total, business, medical, charity and military moving miles, amounts at IRS rates, parking and tolls, business use %, logged car costs for reference and a record check
  • Dashboard: business miles, business mileage amount, business parking and tolls, business use %, trips logged, trips to fix, car costs logged, services due or due soon (with a list), miles by month and category with a chart, and a summary for each vehicle with average MPG
