    What’s inside

    • Dashboard: budget vs spent for 9 categories (gifts, food and hosting, decorations, travel, cards and postage, wrapping and shipping, giving, outings, other), left to spend, days to Christmas, a per-paycheck amount and gift progress by status
    • Gift Budget by Person: a budget for each person; gifts listed, spent, left and status (no gifts listed, nothing bought, in progress, all set, over budget) fill in from the Gift List
    • Gift List: who it is for, the idea, where to buy, price paid, status from idea to given, order date, a need-it-by date and days left (flagged when 7 or fewer)

    • Meal & Hosting: menus for two holiday meals, who is bringing what, oven temps and times, and a shopping list with an estimated total compared with your food budget
    • Turkey Planner: enter the weight and when you want to eat; it shows the latest day to move the turkey to the fridge, a cold-water thaw estimate, the roasting time range at 325°F and when it should go in the oven, based on USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service charts
