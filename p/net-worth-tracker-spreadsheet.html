What’s inside

  • Settings: your name, an optional "as of" date, the first month and an optional net worth goal and goal date
  • Accounts: 80 rows: account, asset or liability, category, institution, last 4 digits, owner, include (Yes or No) and notes; the latest balance is picked up from Balances
  • Balances: one column per month for 24 months; type each balance as a positive number, debts too
  • Monthly Summary: assets, liabilities, net worth, change, change %, debt-to-asset, accounts with a balance and totals for all 11 categories each month
  • Dashboard: net worth, assets, liabilities, change from last month and since your first month, debt-to-asset, goal, goal progress, still to goal and items to fix, plus totals by category, a net worth chart and accounts by balance, largest first
  • Check cells flag a balance typed as text or as a negative number, a category that does not match the type, more than 4 digits in "last 4" and an account left blank in a month after it had a balance
