Cleaning Schedule and Home Maintenance Planner

Cleaning Schedule and Home Maintenance Planner: A cleaning schedule that does not fall apart after one busy week. Instead of "Monday is bathrooms", every task has a frequency and a Last done date. Type the date when you do it, and the spreadsheet works out when it is due next and whether it is overdue, due today, due soon or up to date.

    What’s inside

    • Cleaning Schedule: 56 editable starter tasks across 9 rooms and areas. Each row has a room, frequency (daily to every 2 years), who does it, minutes, Last done, next due date, days left, status and minutes per week
    • This Week: every cleaning task that is overdue or due in the next 7 days, soonest first, with the total minutes, plus maintenance due in the next 30 days (both windows are editable in Settings)
    • Home Maintenance: 29 starter tasks for safety, heating and cooling, plumbing, appliances, electrical, the roof, windows and the yard, with best season, DIY or pro, next due date, status and what you spent this year. Notes cite USFA, CPSC and ENERGY STAR guidance where it applies
    • Maintenance Log: pick the task from a dropdown and type the date, who did it and the cost. The latest logged date counts as that task's Last done (if it is later than the date typed on Home Maintenance), and costs add up by year
    • Dashboard: overdue, due today, due soon, % on schedule, minutes due this week, tasks done in the last 7 days, cleaning status by room with a chart, the next 10 maintenance tasks and upkeep spent this year
    • Chore Chart: for each of up to 8 people, tasks assigned, minutes per week, share of the work, due in the next 7 days, overdue and done in the last 7 days, with a chart
    • Supplies: mark each item Have, Low or Out. Low and Out items show on a shopping list with an estimated total from the prices you type
    • Home Info: filter size, shutoff locations and contacts, plus appliances with warranty status and age
