Our story

Built at a kitchen table

Batch & Build started with a young family trying to keep up. Bills came due, groceries ran short before payday, and the calendar always seemed full.

So they started making their own tools: a paycheck budget, a meal prep plan, a simple tracker for the week. They kept the ones that worked and shared them. That’s still how we make every template, built for busy, everyday life.

A small shop making printable planners, trackers, spreadsheet templates and guides for everyday life, sold as digital downloads.

Where do I buy?

Every product links to its page on Gumroad, where checkout and delivery happen.

Are the products physical?

No. They are digital files you download after purchase, then print at home or use on screen.
